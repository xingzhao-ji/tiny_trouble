## Observed baseline and bounded decisions

The [0.2.5 Workshop audit](../evidence/platform_refinement/workshop_mobile_025/VISUAL_REVIEW.md) reports **732 assertions, 71 failures and 20 reviewed stills**. Seventy repeated font failures represent one defect; one injected Enter probe establishes the second. The Godot editor executable hosted the unchanged exported 0.2.5 PCK through `--main-pack`; this was **not the distributed app executable**. [The report](../evidence/platform_refinement/workshop_mobile_025/capture_report.json) retains executable/PCK identity, fixtures and actual viewport dimensions.

| Defect | Observed cause and consequence | Implemented correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UI-027-04: saved text scale | Workshop loads 1.25, but HUD retains 1.0. Most labels/status/actions stay at 24 logical font units instead of 30. The heading already changes from 30 to 37, so blindly applying another multiplier risks double scaling. | Passes the loaded preference to HUD and retain unscaled per-node font bases. Apply the mobile minimum and saved multiplier once. Preserve the existing integer conversion: body/action 24→30 and heading 30→37 at 1.25. Repeated layout and menu rebuilding must not accumulate scale. |
| UI-027-05: navigation behind portrait guidance | Injected Enter changes Workshop to Crumb Crossing while the rotation guard is visible. The portrait still alone does not prove this behavior. | Guards Workshop navigation commands before transition/audio/scene side effects, covering keyboard and button/command paths. Once landscape returns, an explicit new action can navigate normally; rotation itself must not navigate. |

The current menu, camera, drawer and art remain the baseline for this refinement. The centered mobile menu covers much of the roof label, right drawer and workbench while Pip remains visible. Whether those remaining workshop cues feel sufficient is an **unverified future human composition question**. No redesign or preference claim is authorized by the two functional failures.

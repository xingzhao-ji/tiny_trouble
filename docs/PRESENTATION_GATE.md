## Owner and player acceptance boundary

Human enjoyment, satisfaction and demand are unverified, with no invented sessions. Use [the player protocol and decision ledger](PLAYER_VALIDATION.md) for recurring contextual feedback. Tests demonstrate behavior under recorded conditions, not satisfaction; one player does not establish a market.

**Normal-size human visual judgment is mandatory:** inspect Pip/prop/environment/UI proportions, cohesion and aesthetics at the intended display size without zoom. Use actual devices when available and review both motion and stills for floating, clipping, stretching, clutter and empty space. Numeric ratios and bounds cannot prove that a scene looks right; no automatic beauty score closes this gate. Close-ups remain diagnostic. Compare variants in the same scene/action/display context, record reasons and disagreement, and retain unavailable human/device review as unverified.

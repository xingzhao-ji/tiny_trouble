# Visual reference study — existing-scene presentation gate

Reviewed 2026-09-09 for TT-028. This study uses seven directly inspected, unchanged official reference stills selected for the local Figma board, one additional inspected but excluded crossover still, and the specific Tiny Trouble captures below. Creator statements, observations and proposed changes are distinguished. No reference footage or audio was played or auditioned. The study does not establish animation timing, a game's overall quality, or Tiny Trouble's equivalence to these productions.

W01S04 art is paused as unverified WIP. Presentation work applies first to existing scenes. Pip's cream body, teal feet, Scale Spool, handmade breakfast objects and solo, untimed resizing puzzles remain the identity. The references inform composition, material recognition, posing and information hierarchy; their characters, environments, symbols and music are not production assets.

## Monument Valley: compose the mechanism

**Inspected stills.** The [official level image](../design/reference_study/monument_valley_level_official.png) separates light walking planes from darker vertical faces. Open background cuts through the architecture, making gaps and the central crossing visible. Coral concentrates on the top destination and the lower four-armed control. The white character remains identifiable on a darker bridge. This is a view of the authored arrangement, not proof that a viewer can solve its optical puzzle. [ustwo's release article and image](https://ustwo.com/blog/monument-valley-out-now/)

The [official character composition](../design/reference_study/monument_valley_official.jpg) places a white silhouette on a warm pedestal inside a broad, cool, relatively empty field. Its background layers converge around the subject. The website calls it a screenshot; it is used here for composition, not as evidence of traversal. [ustwo's project page](https://ustwo.com/work/monument-valley/)

**Creator intent.** ustwo describes composing each screen as artwork, designing for people unfamiliar with complex game conventions, adding levels for distinct ideas, and using restrained art, text, sound and animation to invite emotional engagement. These are documented intentions, not measurements of universal player response. [ustwo, 3 April 2014](https://ustwo.com/blog/monument-valley-out-now/)

**Transfer.** Give a Tiny Trouble scene three readable roles: the object changing, the safe surface becoming available and the destination. Use value, silhouette and surrounding space together. Keep a resize or hinge point visible while the object changes. Preserve the household setting and physically authored contacts; no impossible-architecture treatment is proposed.

## It Takes Two: make scale and intention visible

**Inspected stills.** In the [official arcade image](../design/reference_study/it_takes_two_arcade_official.jpg), body-height controls, thick cables, large upholstery weave and the characters' hands braced around joysticks establish miniature scale. The characters have different bends through knees, backs and arms; the objects visibly invite bodily effort. The bright screen anchors the background action. In the [official book image](../design/reference_study/it_takes_two_book_official.jpg), a broad open-arm silhouette, separated brows, wide eyes and an open mouth convey an emphatic pose. Cloth, paper and rigid trim have visibly different construction. Both are publisher stills; neither establishes anticipation, easing or acting continuity. [EA's official image gallery](https://www.ea.com/games/it-takes-two/it-takes-two/buy)

**Creator intent.** In EA's announcement, Hazelight founder and director Josef Fares describes connecting narrative and gameplay mechanics. The announced premise turns humans into dolls and ordinary surroundings into challenges. This supports making a household object's recognizable function drive the visual action. [EA / Hazelight announcement, 10 December 2020](https://news.ea.com/press-releases/press-releases-details/2020/EA-and-Hazelight-Announce-It-Takes-Two-Coming-to-Consoles-and-PC-on-March-26-2021/default.aspx)

**Transfer.** Keep Pip's simpler rig and give each important action a readable intention: look toward the target, brace before effort, respond when it settles. Build paper, baked food and cutlery from a few visible construction cues at the actual camera distance. Do not add dense miniature surface noise merely because this reference supports close views. Full-body expressiveness is a direction to test in motion, not a claim that current stills reveal a timing defect.

## Animal Crossing: cohesive objects and contextual communication

**Inspected stills.** The [official resident frame](../design/reference_study/animal_crossing_residents_official.jpg) keeps two large character heads above one pale dialogue area. A small name tab, dark body text and a colored name establish a clear hierarchy. Rounded logs, door, grass edge and characters share softened proportions. The [official crafting-area frame](../design/reference_study/animal_crossing_craft_official.jpg) uses visible wood, metal and cloth cues inside the same simplified object language. The open-armed character has clear space in front; tools and furniture form small clusters around that space. These images support observations about form and layout, not control reach or dialogue pacing. [Nintendo's official New Horizons page](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/animal-crossing-new-horizons-switch/)

A third inspected [current home image](../design/reference_study/animal_crossing_home_official.jpg) contains a branded LEGO crossover. It is retained with provenance but excluded from the selected board: it is less useful for defining Tiny Trouble's original household materials. The current publisher page includes updated content; these are not asserted to be the original 2020 launch screenshots.

**Creator intent.** In a 2016 series interview, producers Katsuya Eguchi and Hisashi Nogami describe family communication, residents with recognizable personalities and designing around anticipated player responses. They emphasize emotional engagement and positive immersion. This is series history from the New Leaf period, not a New Horizons production interview. [Nintendo's creator interview, 25 November 2016](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/News/2016/November/Learn-more-about-the-development-of-the-Animal-Crossing-series-in-our-interview--1159916.html)

**Transfer.** Use Tiny Trouble's existing rounded type and cards, with one immediate instruction and contextual feedback that leaves the acting character visible. Give crumb and cereal friends a small consistent response vocabulary rather than expanding dialogue volume. Harmonize edge softness and material finish across paper, ceramic and food. Quiet ambience remains a proposed quality; it was not heard in this study.

## Selected board directions and remaining gates

1. **Compose a clear action.** Keep Pip, active prop, transformation pivot and useful landing legible together. Use large value groups and spare space before decorative detail. The Monument Valley and Overcooked stills support this direction.
2. **Show household construction and effort.** Preserve rounded toys while clarifying paper folds, baked edges, ceramic thickness and silver highlights. Add intention through Pip's existing rig. It Takes Two provides visible scale/pose examples; its timing is unreviewed here.
3. **Use contextual, comfortable information.** Retain the token contract and accessibility controls; reduce stale card height and protect the actor during transient feedback. Animal Crossing provides one-message composition, not a template to copy.
4. **Review motion and sound as their own work.** Compare complete resize, refusal, boarding and landing events in actual engine playback, including reduced motion. Write listening notes only after a real audition path is available. A decoded file, source code, still or waveform cannot close an emotional or comedic timing gate.

The companion [audio and feedback study](REFERENCE_STUDY_AUDIO_FEEDBACK.md) supplies the inspected Overcooked publisher image and the Stardew Valley composer-catalog observation. The latter establishes seasonal/location/character organization only; reference audio has not been auditioned. The local board repeats this limitation. No melodies, recordings, characters or reference environments enter the game export.

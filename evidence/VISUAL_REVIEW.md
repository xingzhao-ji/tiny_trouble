Rounded silhouettes, palette and material families are consistent across the six views.

The final captures verify the three previously pending fixes: occupied socket markers disappear, focused completion text uses slate ink, and cinematic target/landing messages match the current state. Remaining visible limitations are thin, pale unoccupied world labels against the warm table, and very small picnic details; the larger HUD supplies their actionable text.

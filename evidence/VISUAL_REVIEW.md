# VS01 visual review

Reviewed actual Godot captures at 1920 × 1080, September 9, 2026. This review covers visible consistency and specific defects; it does not establish subjective quality or uninterrupted motion quality. `ASSET_MANIFEST.json` records the exact inspected capture hashes. The eight menu captures also cover 1280 × 720 and 1920 × 1080; their layout measurements are in `ui_layout_report.json`.

Resolved defects verified in these captures include overexposed main materials, elongated eye animation, inadequate spoon handle width/contact height, and insufficient carton window clearance. The geometric sweep in `flight_clearance.json` independently reports at least 0.0852 units clearance through the window, including conservative Pip stretch and bob. No missing mesh, missing material, debug overlay or obvious body-through-window intersection appears in the reviewed stills. Rounded silhouettes, palette and material families are consistent across the six views.

Three objective issues in the reviewed captures have fixes in the current source: `sync_props()` hides occupied socket markers, the button theme sets `font_focus_color` to slate ink, and cinematic target/landing messages were updated. Final packaged replay captures are pending review for these changes; the older images are not evidence that the fixes render correctly. Still frames do not verify flicker, elastic settling, every moving contact or all supported input paths. The milk strips provide high-contrast route boundaries, but their regular silhouettes and nearly flat white interiors communicate an abstract hazard more strongly than spilled liquid.

Compare against [the art sheet](../assets/reference/art_sheet_concept.png) and [breakfast target](../assets/reference/breakfast_target_concept.png), both explicitly generated concepts. The runtime preserves Pip's identity, the fixed palette, toy silhouettes, broad highlights and warm tabletop framing. It simplifies resin microtexture, cloth weave, paper grain, wood variation, indirect lighting and background dressing. The target uses a lower, closer camera; this is a visual-direction comparison rather than a matched-camera fidelity claim. Texture sophistication and set richness are still below the aspirational target. Charm, composition, humor, perceived craft and enjoyment remain human judgments.

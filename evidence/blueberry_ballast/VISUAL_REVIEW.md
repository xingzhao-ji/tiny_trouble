Two geometry findings are closed for the tested route. The original low and one-berry ramp endpoints entered the box footprint; runtime now shifts the box right and adds a separate fold-out lip. Low endpoints remain below the lip without entering the solid body, while the balanced endpoint joins it. Separate geometry testing found approximately 0.099 m of toe penetration at the first ramp step, missed by the earlier mid-ramp still. The stage now supplies a `SlopePose` parent and per-foot contact lifts using actual cached mesh vertices, without editing the shared Pip asset. The new entry/crossing captures and zero-depth scene check verify that correction in the sampled route; they do not certify every possible animated contact.

The footer reads “Roll / Inspect.” Discovery explains the visible mechanism without exposing internal mass values. All eight earlier [1920-pixel source captures](../ballast_1920/replay_report.json) and eight [1280-pixel alternate-solution captures](../ballast_alternate/replay_report.json) were also inspected, each with ten passing route checks. They establish both-resolution layout and the small-far-berry solution, but precede the final foot-pose correction. Earlier images in this review's directory retain historical copy and are not the final packaged evidence.

The runtime large blueberry radius is about 0.6. The isolated [art studio](../ballast_art/README.md) checked larger radius-0.92 berries against pocket clearance; that is asset capacity evidence, not the stage's authored size. The pan, exposed weights, paper ramp and breakfast graphic retain the cream/amber/coral/teal palette. Five weights remain countable and the three-thread mechanism reads at gameplay distance. Fine scoring and the breakfast face remain subtle; broad shapes carry most of the meaning. The generated [art sheet](../../assets/reference/art_sheet_concept.png) and [breakfast target](../../assets/reference/breakfast_target_concept.png) are aspirational references, with richer material texture and indirect light than the procedural runtime.

No missing mesh/material, debug overlay or clipped menu action appears in the nine final views.

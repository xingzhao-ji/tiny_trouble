# Tiny Trouble backlog

The integrating frontier is VS01 Toast Express Demo (M1), with its M0 foundation being built alongside it. This record describes current missing deliverables and earned evidence; it does not mark a milestone complete. M2 begins only after the applicable VS01 gates pass and unavailable external checks are explicitly recorded. Main campaign stages in `campaign_manifest.json` are plans.

Statuses: **proposed**, **active**, **verified**, **blocked**, **deferred**. A verified subtask does not verify its whole milestone. Human review is recorded separately below.

## Pending human validation

Human review of charm, composition, enjoyment, spontaneous experimentation and first-play duration is unverified. The VS01 8–12 minute duration is a hypothesis. This pending review does not prevent objective fixes, and it does not become a technical pass.

## Out of scope

Multiplayer, procedural campaign replacement, live AI companions/dialogue, crafting/combat systems, monetization, accounts, public leaderboards, new campaign settings and an unrelated editor remain deferred. No public deployment, paid capacity or external messaging is authorized by these records.
